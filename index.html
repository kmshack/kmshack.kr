About

Sixteen years of making Android apps feel right — pixel-perfect where it counts, fast where it matters, invisible everywhere else.

My work sits where UI/UX design meets implementation: interfaces that survive a designer's zoom-in, and frame budgets that survive a mid-range phone. Startup time, memory, motion — the unglamorous parts users feel but never name.

Outside of that, I build indie apps to test ideas end to end, and I write about what breaks along the way. The blog has been running long enough to be an archive of the Android platform itself.
